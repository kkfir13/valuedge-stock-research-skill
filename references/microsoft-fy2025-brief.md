### Read-through

Operating cash flow grew 14.9% and net income 15.5% year over year, while PP&E additions rose 45.1%. Receivables were a sizeable cash use, partly offset by unearned revenue. The company described adding more than two gigawatts of data-center capacity and expanding cloud and AI infrastructure; the report does not isolate AI-only capex or returns, so the full capex increase cannot be assigned to AI. SBC remains material even with a nearly flat diluted weighted-average share count. The reported cash and investment balance exceeds reported debt, but the proxy excludes leases and other obligations. No current price or sourced forecast/discount assumptions are included, so this example makes no fair-value estimate.
